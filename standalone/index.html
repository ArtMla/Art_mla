About

Field engineer turned data scientist.

Five years of hands-on industrial systems work, now paired with a Master's in Data Science — the combination that lets me build models that survive contact with real hardware.

I'm a Data Science graduate (M.Sc., Distinction) and Mechatronics Engineer (B.Eng.) based in Berlin, with over five years of experience across industrial systems — from Siemens S7 PLC control rooms to fibre-optic networks to 100+ solar installations.

My M.Sc. thesis developed a hybrid 1D-CNN + Random Forest predictive maintenance framework for industrial asset failure detection, achieving 97.6% ROC-AUC on binary classification and 0.861 Macro F1 on multiclass fault diagnosis using the AI4I 2020 dataset.

I'm looking for a junior data science role where I can apply a hybrid engineering and data-driven approach to real-world industrial problems.
